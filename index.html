Vision Screening Test

A calibrated, at-home estimate of distance visual acuity using a tumbling “E”.

Step 1 of 3

Set up the test

  1. Use this in Safari with your iPhone held upright.
  2. Set screen brightness high enough that the symbol is crisp, with no glare.
  3. Wear your normal distance glasses or contacts if you want to test your corrected vision.
  4. Test one eye at a time. Cover the other eye without pressing on it.
  5. Keep the phone at the selected distance. A 2.0 m distance works well indoors.


Step 2 of 3

Calibrate your screen

Place a standard bank/credit card against the screen. Adjust the rectangle until its width exactly matches your card (85.60 mm).

Estimated result
20/20
This is a screening estimate, not a diagnosis or prescription. Screen calibration, room lighting, test distance, squinting, dry eyes, and how well the other eye is covered can change the result.

Seek a professional eye exam if one eye is clearly worse than the other, your vision has recently changed, you have eye pain, flashes/floaters, a curtain/shadow in your vision, or you are concerned despite a good home result.

How the score works

“20/40” means that at 20 feet, you can resolve detail that a person with standard 20/20 acuity could resolve at 40 feet. This test uses the same angular sizing principle, scaled to the shorter distance you choose.
